A bug-fix release: two playback problems found by stress-testing the application.

## Fixed

- **Skipping through many passages quickly no longer leaves a long silence.** Pressing Next (or
  Previous) many times in a row made Rail Narrator speak every passage skipped past, one after
  another, before the one you stopped on: after 60 quick presses the silence lasted 20 to 45
  seconds. Now only the passage you stop on is spoken, and reading resumes in under a second.
- **Changing the voice several times in a row no longer stops the reading.** The status stayed at
  "Synthesising speech" with nothing playing until you pressed Cancel and Play. Reading now carries
  on in the last voice chosen.
