A bug-fix release: short freezes of the window, found by stress-testing the application and
measured with a new diagnostic.

## Fixed

- **Changing the speed no longer freezes the window.** Each change froze it for up to a fifth of a
  second while the passage was prepared again at the new speed. Several quick changes are now
  applied as one, at the speed chosen last.
- **Starting a passage no longer freezes the window.** Play, Replay, Next and Previous while
  paused, and Play after Cancel, froze it for up to a sixth of a second on a long passage.
- **Opening the review notes for the first time no longer freezes the window.** The first opening
  in a session froze it for up to a fifth of a second while fonts for the notes' styles and their
  maths symbols were looked up.
- **Saving a preference no longer touches the disk on the window's thread.** Speed, zoom, the
  sidebar, colours and the voice are saved in the background, and your place in the recent
  documents list is too. They are all saved before the application closes.

## For testing

With `RN_FOLLOW_LOG=1`, the log of the reading view now notes every pause of the window over
50 ms with what the .NET runtime was doing at the time (garbage collection, compiling,
waiting on a lock). See `docs/testing-real-world.md`.

## Known limitations

As before: English only; the Windows builds have not yet been run on a Windows PC; the optional
forced aligner is Linux-only and installed from source; pages are shown one at a time. A rotated
scanned page can still have a phrase read twice where two sideways lines meet.
