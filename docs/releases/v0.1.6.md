A small release: you come back to the right place after the narration changes, and a document
with a saved narration opens sooner.

## Fixed

- **Your place in a document is kept when its narration is prepared again.** This happens after
  an update, after installing a layout model, or after changing what is read (references,
  footnotes, captions). Until now, the reading could resume at a different passage, or at the
  start. The reader now goes back to the same sentence on the page. Measured on a 27-page paper,
  the old way put the reader in the wrong passage for 214 of 552 passages after a filter change,
  and for 518 of 547 after installing a layout model. A place saved by 0.1.5 or earlier is
  restored the old way one last time.

## Improved

- **A document with a narration saved beside it opens sooner.** The narration already has
  everything needed to play, so the pages are now read for notes after the document opens,
  instead of before. On a 27-page paper it was ready in 2.2 s instead of 4.8 s, and more is
  saved on a slower computer or a scanned document. A note or highlight made in those first few
  seconds is asked to wait a moment.

checksums.

## Known limitations

As before: English only; the Windows builds have not yet been run on a Windows PC; the optional
forced aligner is Linux-only and installed from source; pages are shown one at a time. A rotated
scanned page can still have a phrase read twice where two sideways lines meet. "the point a (the
origin) lies" is still read with the article, and "(i) A model" with the letter.
