A small bug-fix release.

## Fixed

- **Intervals after "a value in" or a single letter are kept.** With citations off (the default),
  "x takes a value in [1, 5]" and "for k in [1, 5]" were read as "x takes a value in" and "for k
  in": the brackets were taken for citation numbers. They are now read. Citations written the
  same way, such as "as shown in [12]" or "the proof is in [3, 7]", are still left out.

The 0.1.7 notes said that "values in [1, 5]" was still dropped. That was wrong: 0.1.7 already
read it. The cases above were the ones still dropped.

Because what is read has changed, documents are prepared again the first time they are opened,
and their speech is made again.

## Known limitations

As before: English only; the Windows builds have not yet been run on a Windows PC; the optional
forced aligner is Linux-only and installed from source; pages are shown one at a time. With
citations off, a bracket after "is in" or "from" ("is in [1, 5]", "a draw from [1, 5]") is still
left out, because citations are written the same way ("the proof is in [12]", "adapted from
[12]").
